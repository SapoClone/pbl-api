## 3. Scenarios walked through the code

1. **Two shifts touching at 16:00 (08:00-16:00 and 16:00-22:00, same person, same day).** assertNoOverlap uses `startA < endB && endA > startB`: 08<22 and 16>16 false, so no overlap. Accepted. Tested (service.spec:136). On-duty at exactly 16:00 returns only the second shift.
2. **Overnight shift 22:00-06:00.** assertOrdered: 1320 >= 360, so 422 V000 "endTime must be later than startTime". Must be entered as 22:00-23:59 (day N) plus 00:00-06:00 (day N+1). Minute 23:59 is on neither row (end excluded) on day N.
3. **Member in two branches on the same day.** Branch A 08:00-12:00 and branch B 13:00-17:00: allowed. Branch B 11:00-15:00: S001, because the overlap check loads all the person's rows with no branch filter (create:92-107). On-duty for branch B at 14:00 lists them; branch A at 14:00 does not.
4. **Cashier opens a register outside their scheduled shift (or with no schedule at all).** Succeeds (201) if they hold pos.create and the register's branch is in their scope. No schedule lookup exists (BR-SH-17).
5. **Two cashiers on one register.** By registerId (screen contract): the second gets 409 SHIFT_ALREADY_OPEN (tested). By registerCode (POST /pos/shifts/open without registerId, default "REG-01"): both succeed, since the unique index and check include staff_id. Then both can ring sales into their own shift.
6. **Cashier B rings a sale with cashier A's shiftId.** Pre-flight and in-transaction check see shift OPEN and the branch matching; staff_id is never compared. Order: created_by = B, shift_id = A's shift. The cash goes to A's expected cash. A's variance at close will be off by B's cash sales. staff-sales by seller says B, by shift says A.
7. **Order rung up with no open shift.** PosCheckoutDto.shiftId is optional. With none, checkout completes; order.shift_id NULL; cash is added to no drawer (line 1600 guard); it appears in staff-sales (by created_by) but under no shiftId.
8. **Close a shift with active drafts.** Close succeeds. Contract promises 400 S022 with the draft ids; the code never queries pos_drafts. The draft stays ACTIVE with the old shift_id; checking it out with that shift gives 409 SHIFT_NOT_OPEN; if the UI strips shiftId it sells with no shift (scenario 7).
9. **Close with a cash shortage.** Opening 500,000, cash sales 300,000, actual 795,000: expected 800,000 and variance -5,000, stored, event emitted. Finance (async) posts debit Cash variance 5,000 / credit Cash on hand 5,000 and an issue/voucher. The shift closes anyway; nobody is charged and no approval is needed. An overage of 5,000 credits Other income.
10. **Close with a checkout still in flight.** 409 SHIFT_HAS_PENDING_TRANSACTIONS with pendingCheckoutId; nothing changes (tested, Test J).
11. **Close someone else's shift.** A manager (or any cashier with pos.update, plus pos.create on the screen route) in scope closes a colleague's shift; shift.staff_id stays the opener, closed_by = actor. A cashier who lacks pos.update cannot close even their own shift on the /pos route (permission-level, not verified which seeded roles have it).
12. **Return of an item sold by a former employee.** Employee removed (member.remove deletes schedules and memberships, sessions revoked). Their orders keep created_by = their uuid (no FK). Return flow checks only the order status; works. The staff-sales rows for that uuid still show, but the members list no longer resolves a name.
13. **Employee removed while their POS shift is open.** Platform and sales do not talk: the shift stays OPEN, the register is blocked for others (registerId route) until someone closes it; the finance variance is attributed to closed_by in the journal postedBy? No: finance uses payload.staffId (the opener) as the actor (reconciliation.service.ts:884), so a removed person is shown as the poster of a shortage they did not close. Another person closing it is only in sales' closed_by.
14. **On-duty answer at the exact end minute.** Shift 08:00-16:00 at 16:00:00+07 (09:00Z): minute 960, `960 < 960` false: not on duty. At 15:59:59 (minute 959): on duty. Start minute included. Tested (service.spec:390).
15. **Manager restricted to branch A asks for branch B.** /staff-schedules?branchId=B and /on-duty?branchId=B: 404. PATCH/DELETE of a B shift: 404. POST for branch B: 404 (canAccessBranch). /pos/shifts?branchId=B: 404 SHIFT_NOT_FOUND; /staff-sales?branchId=B: rejected by assertBranchAllowed. Without a filter only A's rows come back.
16. **Refund a week-old cash sale.** returns.service picks the original (closed) shift; the UPDATE has no status check; the old shift's expected_cash falls and cash_refunds_total rises after it was closed and reconciled. Stored variance and the finance journal are not updated, so today's drawer is physically short with no record. By contrast the screen refund route (order-actions.service.ts:437-481) only moves an OPEN shift's cash.
17. **Refund of an order in staff-sales.** Seller S sold 1,000,000; customer returns it all next day. Order stays COMPLETED, so S still shows 1,000,000 revenue and 1 order. The refunding cashier gets nothing negative either.
18. **A sale at 23:50 UTC+7 on Nov 5 completes at 00:10 Nov 6.** Report counts it in Nov 5 (created_at). Not verified whether created_at is the cart time or the completion time for POS (probably the time the order row was written at finalize; not verified).
19. **Locked (suspended) member.** Schedule rows stay (BR-SH-14), so on-duty still lists them; their sessions are revoked on lock? (not verified), so they cannot sell.
20. **Removing a manager with schedules.** member.remove deletes every staff_schedule row of the person in the store in one transaction; re-adding the person gives them no shifts and past on-duty history cannot be recovered (there is none: the schedule is weekly, not a dated roster).

## 4. Defects and gaps (ranked by business impact)

1. **Sales can be rung up with no shift, or into another person's shift** (BR-SH-28, BR-SH-29). checkout.dto.ts:63-65 optional shiftId; checkout.service.ts:1745-1790 does not compare shift.staff_id. Money in the drawer ends up untraceable, a cashier's variance can be caused by someone else. Fix: for channel POS require shiftId, and require shift.staff_id = caller unless the caller has an override permission.
2. **S022 promised but not built** (BR-SH-26). shifts.service.closeShift:289-309 never reads pos_drafts. Fix: add a query for ACTIVE drafts with that shift_id and throw 400 S022 with the ids (or auto-discard them with the close note).
3. **Cash refund of an old sale rewrites a closed shift** (BR-SH-34). returns.service.ts:583, 640-650. Fix: take the refund from the caller's current open shift (or require status OPEN on the UPDATE and else book to the open shift of the actor), and refuse a refund of cash with no open shift.

## 5. Open questions for the business owner

1. Must every POS sale belong to an open shift? Recommended: yes for channel POS (reject with SHIFT_NOT_OPEN), no for online and manual back-office orders.
2. May a cashier sell into a shift opened by someone else (a shared drawer)? Recommended: no; a manager with an override permission may.
3. Do you have shifts that cross midnight? Recommended: yes, support them (allow end earlier than start meaning next day).
4. Should opening a register by someone who is not scheduled at that hour be refused, warned, or allowed? Recommended: warn and record, do not block (the weekly roster has no swaps or days off yet).
5. Should only the duty cashier or shift_lead be allowed to open a register? Recommended: no, keep permission-based; use duty for reporting only.
6. What is the commission base: gross grand total (as now), after discount, or net of returns and refunds? Recommended: net of returns, by the date of the refund, before shipping and tax.
7. Who is responsible for a cash shortage: the opener, the closer, or the shop? Recommended: the shift opener (staff_id); allow a tolerance of 0 VND and require a close note above a threshold (what number? suggested 50,000 VND).
8. When a member is locked, should they leave the on-duty list and the schedule? Recommended: leave the on-duty list immediately, keep the rows.
9. May a manager schedule someone in a branch the person is not assigned to? Recommended: no (reject).
10. Should closing a shift with active drafts be blocked (contract S022) or auto-discard the drafts? Recommended: block with S022 as the contract says.
11. May two people hold open shifts on the same register code? Recommended: no, never.
12. Who may close another person's shift: any holder of pos.update, or only the branch manager? Recommended: opener or a manager role; record closed_by (already done).
13. When a shift of a removed employee is still open, should the removal be refused? Recommended: yes (refuse until it is closed).
14. Should a refund of a cash sale after the shift closed take cash from today's drawer? Recommended: yes, and refuse a cash refund when the refunding cashier has no open shift.
